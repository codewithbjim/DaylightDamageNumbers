See what you hit for. Daylight UI's combat text shows the hits you take and the heals you get. Damage Numbers adds the damage you deal: every hit on your target lands in a stream, in your number style, with crits bigger.

Damage Numbers is a plugin for Daylight UI on WoW: Forever. It fits right into Daylight UI: its settings have their own page under Plugins in Daylight UI's options, with the same switches, streams and colors as Daylight UI's own messages.

Setup

Install it next to Daylight UI and log in. Your hits show in the Right stream. Type /dui dmg to see a few sample hits.

Settings

Open /dui > Plugins > Damage Numbers. Each message has a switch, a stream and a color:

  • Your melee and ranged hits: weapon hits on your target, and your pet's.
  • Your spell hits: colored by school when Daylight UI's school colors are on.
  • Your crits in their own color: off, a crit keeps its hit's color. It always lands bigger.
  • Your misses: Dodge, Parry, Miss, Resist and the rest, as a word.

Two more switches sit below them:

  • Only when solo: in a group, the game reports other players' hits on your target too. This hides your messages while you're grouped.
  • Hide the game's damage numbers: the numbers popping off your target would only double up, so they go, but only once your first real hit has shown in a stream. Turn it off and you get back what you had.

Good to know

  • WoW: Forever gives addons no combat log, so only hits on your current target show.
  • If the game ever stops reporting your hits while its own numbers are hidden, Damage Numbers puts them back for the session and says so in chat.

Requires

  • Daylight UI, with its Combat text switched on.

Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Typing /dui dmgdebug and pasting what it says helps a lot.
